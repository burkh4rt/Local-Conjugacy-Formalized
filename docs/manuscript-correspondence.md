Both structural branches of Lemma 1.2 use the paper's minimal obstruction
arguments. Chain intersections and neighborhood extension appear in
[ZornRestriction](../LocalConjugacy/ZornRestriction.lean); the branches are
[PronilpotentZorn](../LocalConjugacy/PronilpotentZorn.lean) and
[SupersolvableRestriction](../LocalConjugacy/SupersolvableRestriction.lean).
Injectivity is established before it is used to reflect invariance of a lift.

Proposition 2.2 proves the needed fiberwise coprime argument directly instead of
using an unformalized inflation–restriction sequence. In Proposition 4.1, the
abelian complement input cited from Shin is supplied by an independent
finite-quotient cocycle norm proof. These are proof-level differences; neither is
a literature axiom or an extra hypothesis in the compared theorem. The remaining
main arguments follow finite-kernel induction and the paper's G/(N∩U) compactness
reduction. Older alternative finite-ambient proofs remain supporting lemmas, not
substitutes for the current numbered statements.

Concrete counterexample computations use ordinary kernel-checked `decide`. The
Heisenberg group law is written in the three upper-unitriangular matrix
coordinates; the isomorphism with the constructed subgroup is proved. The
quaternion action is realized by explicit invertible 2×2 matrices over F₃, and
its map onto GL(2,3) is proved bijective.
